How it works

Three steps, then it disappears.

01

One command

Paste curl -fsSL https://tannnnnnnnnnnnn.github.io/Quill/install.sh | sh into Terminal. Quill lands in your menu bar and checks that recording works. No bots to invite into your meetings.

02

It hears the call

Zoom, Meet, a phone on speaker — Quill catches your mic and your Mac's audio on separate tracks, then transcribes both on-device.

03

Notes, ready

The moment you hang up, a clean summary with action items lands in your notes folder. Search it, share it, forget it.

Everything you'd ask before installing.

Does it record without asking?

01
Consent & control

No. Quill asks before every recording, shows an indicator while it listens, and stops on one click. It does record everyone on the call, though — many places require their consent first, so ask.

↗

Where does my data live?

02
Privacy

On your Mac, in folders you pick. Audio and transcription run on-device; the only thing that reaches the network is Claude Code writing your note. There is no Quill server and no telemetry.

↗

Which calls does it work with?

03
Compatibility

The Mac app records any audio your Mac can hear — Zoom, Meet, Teams, FaceTime, or a phone on speaker. The Chrome extension detects Google Meet, the Zoom web client, and Teams, each tested on a real call. Webex is written but untested.

↗

What does it cost?

04
Pricing

Nothing. Quill is MIT licensed and always will be. It does lean on Claude Code to write your notes, so that step needs a paid Claude plan.
